New here? Quick-start guide

Build a force, keep your designs, then take them to the table. Use the shortcuts below to find the controls.

  1. Choose your force

    Select a faction, optional affiliation and points limit. Each faction keeps its own saved rosters.

  2. Add units

    Add a premade for a ready-made loadout, or build a custom unit from a chassis, weapon groups and systems. Hover, focus or tap ⓘ for component rules. Southern Cross uses stock imports only.

  3. Save and organize

    Edits save in this browser. Use New roster or Duplicate roster for another force; add tags to find it later. Save custom designs to your library for reuse.

  4. Keep a backup

    Export roster saves a JSON file you can keep or move to another device. Import roster adds a new saved force. Saved roster backups and the unit library have their own export/import controls.

  5. Print your references

    Print roster includes premade and custom profiles. Custom unit cards gives each custom form its own card. Army quick reference gathers the rules used by the selected force.

  6. Track a battle

    Battle tracker starts or resumes a separate snapshot of the selected force. Record Structure, forms, statuses, limited uses and notes. Roster edits do not change an ongoing battle; start a new battle to use the updated force.

Custom units

Southern Cross uses stock imports only. Custom units cannot be added to this faction.

Build a custom unit

Hover a component name or ⓘ for its rules. You can also focus or tap ⓘ.

Chassis values use the source ranges. Components with missing prices and chassis without complete base stats are unavailable. Faction locks apply. Selected subfaction traits are listed with your force; component prices stay unchanged.

Weapon groups

Each row is an independent group. Linked groups combine identical ranged weapons: first three copies at base cost, fourth at twice base, fifth at three times base, and so on.

Transform forms

The main profile defines Mech form. Other profiles are derived automatically from it. Choose weapon availability below; installed systems stay shared. Each component is charged once and linked copy counts stay fixed across forms.

Component costs

    Southern Cross imports

    Stock units only, from Iron Concord, UNMC, and New Avalon. No Cybernetic Rebellion, Heroes, named characters, or Unique units. No customization.

    Each imported mech pays its full component cost, plus a chassis tax and source modifier. It counts as Southern Cross and keeps its stock weapons, systems, and unit rules. Original faction rules, subfaction and detachment bonuses, faction-wide command benefits, and source-locked benefits do not apply.

    Chassis tax: Light +2 · Medium +3 · Heavy +4 · Superheavy / Elite +5.
    Source modifier: Iron Concord +1 · UNMC +3 · New Avalon +5.

    Only complete profiles with a confirmed chassis tax tier are selectable. Named frames without a confirmed tier remain unavailable.
