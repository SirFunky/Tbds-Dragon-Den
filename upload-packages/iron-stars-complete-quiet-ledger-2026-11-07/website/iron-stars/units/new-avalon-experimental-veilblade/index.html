Systems, traits & special rules

Systems & traits

Veil Cloak Overcharger

Veil Cloak Overcharger – Instead of normal cloak, may activate a hard phase: becomes untargetable until next activation.
Drawback: On deactivation, roll 1D6 → on a 1, take 1 STR (“feedback shock”).

Neural Reflex Suite (Experimental)

Neural Reflex Suite (Experimental) – Always reroll 1 failed Defence die.
Drawback: On natural 1s, take 1 STR (“neural backlash”).

Experimental Overdrive

Experimental Overdrive – May take a 3rd action; if it’s an Attack, gain +1 to hit. On a 1–2, suffer 1 STR. On a 6, system burns out.

Veil Phase+

Veil Phase+ – Cancel first hit and gain +1 ME next melee attack.
