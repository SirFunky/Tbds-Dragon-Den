Systems, traits & special rules

Systems & traits

Relic Might+

Relic Might+ – If stationary, +1 ME and AP +1 (overcharged stance).

Experimental Overdrive

Experimental Overdrive – May take a 3rd action; if it’s an Attack, gain +1 to hit. On a 1–2, suffer 1 STR. On a 6, system burns out (can’t use again this battle).

Last Vow+

Last Vow+ – On destruction, make 1 final melee attack at +1 ME and AP +1.
