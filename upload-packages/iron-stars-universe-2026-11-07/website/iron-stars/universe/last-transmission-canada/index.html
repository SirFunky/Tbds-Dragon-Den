Final Military Transmission — Canadian Defense Network

A command net’s last report as an unknown colossal threat overruns Quebec.

Beyond the front lines

[burst static]
“Sunray, Sunray, this is Bastion Actual, priority traffic— authenticate immediate— authenticate—

Target is confirmed visual. I repeat, target is confirmed visual. This is not a hostile armored column, not an air asset, not any known platform—

[distant concussive impact]

Jesus Christ… it’s enormous.

Grid Quebec-Seven is gone. I repeat, Quebec-Seven is gone. Most of Quebec is—

[overlapping voices]
—say again?
—entire sectors lost!
—fall back, fall back!

Negative, negative, this thing is moving too fast— all units in eastern corridor, break formation and retreat south immediately!

[sirens]
[automatic warning tone]

We have catastrophic losses across the province. Defensive lines are not holding. Armor is ineffective. Air response ineffective. I repeat: all response assets ineffective.

It just crossed another population center— my God—

[violent interference]

Sunray, authorize regional quarantine now. Full collapse conditions. Mark all northern sectors as no-return biohazard zones.

[screaming in background]
[metal tearing]

It’s here— it’s right on top of us—

All stations, all stations, this is Bastion Actual: abandon positions, abandon positions! Run! RUN!

[roaring signal distortion]

Canada Command, do you copy? Canada Command—”

[signal terminated]
