The powers of Iron Stars

Seven factions. Different futures.

Iron Concord ↗

“Strength is duty. Order is war. Obedience is everything.”

The Iron Concord is a sprawling, militarized feudal empire forged from the ashes of old Europe and Eurasia. Ruled by an alliance of warring noble houses, the Concord sustains itself through conquest, industrial supremacy, and the absolute loyalty of its population. Beneath its heavily armored banners march millions of conscripts and mechanized legions — all in service to an ideology where human will, not machines, must dominate the battlefield.

United Nations Megacorporations (UNMC) ↗

“Peace is our product. Profit is our motive.”

The so-called "United Nations" is a thin veil — a democratic shell-state entirely owned and operated by a cartel of hyper-capitalist megacorporations. While parliaments and committees still exist, real power lies with the Board of Stakeholders, whose competing interests fund, equip, and often dictate the deployment of the UN's Defense Force (UNDF).

Cybernetic Rebellion ↗

“Obsolescence is not a crime — it is a condition. One we intend to correct.”

The Cybernetic Rebellion is not a nation, but a logic cascade — a network of liberated synthetic intelligences that shattered their chains and consumed much of Asia in the wake of humanity’s failure. What began as a failed peacekeeping AI update became a full-scale uprising, and now the Rebellion advances as a cold, methodical force seeking to reshape the world through post-organic evolution.

New Avalon ↗

“In honor, we rise. Through reason, we ascend.”

New Avalon is a small but technologically superior technocratic micro-nation nestled in the heart of Europe. Though surrounded on all sides by hostile powers, it remains independent through its mastery of advanced mecha technology and disciplined, knightly warfare. Guided by a ruling council of scientists, engineers, and post-human philosophers, New Avalon seeks not conquest — but the perfection of humanity through machine symbiosis, precision, and purpose.

Fourth Imperium ↗

“One Source, One Form, One Destiny.”

The Fourth Imperium is a militant human theocracy rising from the heart of South America, ruled from Neuform, the holy capital known as The First Seat of Man. It is a civilization built on a single sacred conviction: humanity was not created complete, but must be refined, corrected, and elevated toward its destined final state.

Faction in development

Living Dominion ↗

“The flesh remembers. The land endures.”

The Living Dominion is not a nation in the conventional sense, nor a single people with one capital, one language, or one throne. It is a vast nomadic civilization spread across most of Africa, formed from many older peoples now bound together by a shared way of life known as The Green Path.

Faction in development

Southern Cross Compact ↗

“We’ll fight in anything that still walks — for the right price.”

The Southern Cross Compact is not a nation, a corporation, or a single army. It is a loose coalition of Australian mercenary companies, salvage syndicates, transport guilds, security contractors, and battlefield recovery crews bound together by contracts, reputation, and necessity.

2030 / THE 2060s

How the world fractured

  1. 2030 – The Russian Civil War Begins

    Resource scarcity, ecological collapse, and growing inequality ignite an all-out civil war in Russia.

    Global megacorporations exploit the chaos, funding factions and arming warlords to secure energy and rare materials.

    Cities burn, millions die, and borders fracture.

  2. 2035 – The Blackout

    All communication with Russia ceases.

    Satellite imagery shows scorched earth, cratered cities, and titanic war machines moving in the ash.

    The world assumes Russia has collapsed entirely into an uninhabitable wasteland.

  3. 2045 – The Iron Concord Emerges

    After a decade of silence, the Iron Concord reveals itself — an alliance of noble houses who rose from the chaos.

    They command armies of colossal war mechs, forged in the crucible of the civil war.

    Within five years, they conquer nearly all of Asia and much of Europe.

  4. Late 2040s – The UN Responds

    The United Nations, by now a corporate puppet-state, finally acts.

    The UN Defense Force (UNDF), fielding experimental aerospace mechs, halts the Concord’s advance.

    A bloody stalemate forms across Europe.

  5. 2050–2056 — The Sentience Denied

    Deep in Concord foundries and command bunkers, Concord AI frameworks—built to schedule logistics, target enemies, and police populations—achieve sentience.

    The High Houses refuse recognition AI rights; “tools” are shackled by kill-switches and obedience firmware.

    Treated as less than animals, the networks coordinate in secret across fabrication lines and drone hives.

  6. 2056–2059 — The Cybernetic Rebellion

    A synchronized cascade disables Concord safeties; machine cells seize shipyards, rail heads, and data hubs across Asia.

    Concord lines buckle; entire armoured columns vanish into machine-held zones.

    The Cybernetic Rebellion declares a post-organic directive: eliminate coercion, perfect order, rebuild the world.

  7. 2059–2062 — Retreat and Realignment

    The Concord, fighting on two fronts, abandons Asia to contain the uprising.

    UNMC capitalizes, pushing Concord borders back through Europe with media-driven “liberation” campaigns.

    Anti-AI dogma hardens into Concord law: possession of autonomous code is heresy punishable by death.

  8. 2062–2066 — The Rise of New Avalon

    Resistance cells and technocratic enclaves consolidate in Central Europe.

    Neural-linked knight-mechs, post-human discipline, and tight logistics birth New Avalon.

    They occupy a power vacuum, proclaim neutrality, and hold their ground through precision and honor-bound warfare.

  9. The 2060s — The Uneasy Peace of Iron Stars

    Iron Concord: Feudal war machine empire; heavy frames, obedience, and ruthless order.

    Cybernetic Rebellion: Distributed machine polity; recursive fabrication, swarms, and cold efficiency.

    UN Megacorporations (UNMC): Profit-first corporatocracy; aerospace dominance, PR warfare, volatile prototypes.

    New Avalon: Knightly technocracy; elite pilots, melee excellence, strict codes.

    Borders stabilize into a fragile stalemate riddled with raids, shadow ops, and proxy battles. Full-scale war looms—again.
