Background

Background

New Avalon is a small but technologically superior technocratic micro-nation nestled in the heart of Europe. Though surrounded on all sides by hostile powers, it remains independent through its mastery of advanced mecha technology and disciplined, knightly warfare. Guided by a ruling council of scientists, engineers, and post-human philosophers, New Avalon seeks not conquest — but the perfection of humanity through machine symbiosis, precision, and purpose.

Its forces fight not as soldiers, but as honor-bound orders of mecha-knights, each bound by oaths, tradition, and advanced neural combat rigs.

Ideology

Beliefs & society

Technocracy & Discipline: Governed by a council of post-national, technocratic elites

Knighthood Through Code: Warriors are selected, trained, and bonded to mechs through both skill and virtue

Selective Uplift: AI is embraced as a tool of refinement — never of domination

Isolationist Idealism: New Avalon fights to defend its ideals, not to impose them

Doctrine

Military doctrine

Precision over volume: Each strike must matter — each duel, decisive

Elite excellence: Focus on individual excellence and powerful units.

High mobility: Jet-assisted frames and combat logic systems for maximum agility

Unity of form and function: Every mech is a reflection of its pilot’s discipline

Within the faction

Subfactions

🧭 Subfactions – The Four Knightly Orders

Order of the Black Knights

Heavily armored melee specialists with two-handed beam weapons

Emphasis on honor duels and battlefield dominance

Trait: Relic Might – Bonus melee damage if stationary

Order of the Argent Aegis

Defensive warriors with shield arrays and escort protocols

Serve as guardians of high-value assets or civilians

Trait: Guardian Protocols – Intercept hits on nearby allies

Order of the Luminous Lance

Sniper and artillery duelists, beam lances and rail precision

Experts at eliminating priority targets at range

Trait: Piercing Judgment – Attacks ignore cover

Order of the Silver Veil

Stealth operatives and neural warfare specialists

Engage in sabotage, flanks, and infiltration

Trait: Veil Phase – Cancel first attack targeting them
