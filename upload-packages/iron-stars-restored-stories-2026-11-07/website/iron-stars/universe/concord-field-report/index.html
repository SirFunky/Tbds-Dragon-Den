Iron Concord — Field Report Extract

Lt. Yelena Markovic returns from the Neutral Zone with a damaged mech and a long memory.

Iron Concord

Operative: Lt. Yelena Markovic, Scout Division
Location: Neutral Zone Sector 17-B
Status: Returned (critical systems compromised)
Debrief Timestamp: [REDACTED]

“Behind me, the forest broke apart under Rebellion fire—trees bursting like powderkegs. My comms were gone, my extraction burned, and Brass had left me to rot.

They hunt like algorithms: straight lines, best options, nothing wasted. So I gave them something wasteful. Waited in shadows. Killed from the side. Ran again. Waited in water. Played dead until they overran me.

By the time I dragged myself across the Zone line, my mech bleeding sparks and coolant, the outpost was empty. The bastards had moved on without a word.

That’s Iron Concord command: burn what you must, lose what you must, and expect survivors to find their way home alone.

Whoever signed this op will learn—scouts don’t forget when they’ve been spent like ammunition.”

— Lt. Markovic
