    Chapter 01

    The basics #

    ⚔️ OBJECTIVE #

    Defeat your opponent by completing mission objectives, destroying enemy mechs, and seizing control of key locations.

    Needs review · Objective marker dimensions

    The source gives 25–40 mm diameter and 2–4 cm radius as alternatives. These are different sizes. Agree on your marker size before play.

    Standard Objective Marker Size #

    25 mm to 40 mm diameter base, or

    2–4 cm token radius (round)

    A unit controls the objective if it is within 3 cm of the center and no enemies are also within that range

    Optional: Place a mech part, relay tower, or glowing token for visual flair.

    🏁 End of Game: Victory & Draws #

    At the end of the game, the player with the most Victory Points (VP) wins.

    🤝 In Case of a Draw (Same VP): #

    Use the following tie-breakers in order, unless the mission specifies otherwise:

    Control of More Objectives

    If one player controls more active objectives, they win.

    Total Units Remaining

    The player with more surviving units wins.

    Total Points of Destroyed Enemy Units

    The player who destroyed the most enemy unit points (by cost) wins.

    Last to Destroy a Unit

    If still tied, the player who last destroyed an enemy unit wins.

    Draw Declared

    If all else is tied, the game ends in a draw — no victor, just burning mechs.

    📚 BASIC GAME STRUCTURE #

    Players: 2 or more

    Map Size: 61.0 cm–122.0 cm based on scenario

    Turns: Players alternate activating 1 unit at a time

    Actions per Unit: 2 per activation
    (Move, Attack, Use Ability, Transform, Interact, etc.)

    📊 UNIT STAT PROFILE #

    Each unit has the following:

    Move (M): Distance in cm per Move action

    Defence (D): Target number an attacker must roll to hit

    Structure (STR): Hit points

    Traits: Special rules or passive abilities

    Weapons: Defined by FP (Firepower) or ME (Melee), range, and keywords

    Chapter 02

    Terrain & movement #

    🌍 TERRAIN & MOVEMENT RULES #

    Iron Stars uses centimeters (cm) for all distances. Mechs are powerful, but terrain shapes the battle — smart positioning is key.

    🚶‍♂️ CORE MOVEMENT RULES #

    A unit may move up to its M value (in cm) per Move action

    Mechs may move through friendly units, but not end movement on top of them

    Units may pivot freely without costing movement

    🧱 TERRAIN TYPES #

    🧱 TERRAIN TYPES #
    Terrain TypeMovement EffectCover Effect
    Open Ground#No penaltyNone
    Difficult Ground (mud, snow, debris)#-5 cm movementNone
    Obstacles (fences, pipes, barricades)#Must stop or vault (costs +2 cm)No cover unless taller than half the mech
    Ruins / Rubble#-5 cm movement when insideProvides Cover (+1 Defence vs ranged)
    Buildings / Walls#Impassable unless entered via openingProvides Cover
    Forests / Light Cover#-2 cm movementProvides Cover
    Elevation (hills, platforms)#Climbing up costs +5 cm unless Hover/JetProvides Cover if prone or obscured

    🛡️ COVER RULE (Recap) #

    If a unit is partially obscured by terrain from the attacker’s point of view, it is in Cover

    Cover provides +1 Defence vs ranged attacks

    Cover does not apply to melee attacks

    🔧 Wrecks as Terrain #

    When a mech is destroyed, do not remove it from the table. Instead, leave the model in place as a wreck.

    🧩 Wreck Rules: #

    Counts as Impassable Terrain (cannot move through or stand on)

    Provides Cover (+1 Defence vs ranged attacks) to units within 2.5 cm of it

    Does not block line of sight, but may obscure it (use model silhouette)

    Wrecks can’t be targeted, but may be removed in narrative or campaign missions

    🧠 Optional Advanced Rule: #

    Wreckage is Hazardous — units ending their turn in base contact take 1 STR damage (e.g. overheating reactor, unstable frame)

    Engineer units may salvage a wreck for repair or drone deployment

    🎯 INTENT-BASED TERRAIN USE #

    Before the game starts, players agree on what terrain pieces count as:

    Cover

    Difficult Ground

    Blocking / Impassable

    Climbable / Vaultable

    Keep it clear and fast — you can even mark terrain with symbols or cards if needed.

    🔧 SPECIAL MOVEMENT TRAITS #

    🔧 SPECIAL MOVEMENT TRAITS #
    TraitEffect
    Walker#Ignores Difficult Ground and Rubble penalties
    Hover#Ignores terrain height changes; can move over obstacles
    Jetpack#Ignores all terrain while moving; can fly over buildings and walls
    Burrower#Spends 1 turn underground, then reappears anywhere on the map

    Author-approved addition · 2026-11-07

    Wheeled #

    • +5 cm Move when the entire move stays on Open Ground.
    • Cannot climb elevation.
    • Other terrain uses the normal movement penalties.

    Author-approved addition · 2026-11-07

    Cybernetic Rebellion alternate-form movement #

    • Cybernetic Rebellion alternate forms may choose Walker, Wheeled, Tracked, Hover, Flyer, or Burrower movement.
    • Vehicle and animal forms use the movement type appropriate to how they move.
    • Base Move stays unchanged; there is no blanket vehicle or animal speed bonus. Wheeled gains its +5 cm only when the entire move stays on Open Ground.

    🧠 OPTIONAL TERRAIN RULES #

    🧠 OPTIONAL TERRAIN RULES #
    Rule TypeExample
    Destructible#Walls collapse after enough hits (e.g. 3+ damage)
    Interactive#Terminals, power relays — require an Interact action to use
    Hazards#Lava, toxic zones, EMP fields — deal damage or disable actions
    Low Gravity / Space#Use drift/glide rules (Advanced terrain packs)

    📐 UNIT SIZE & SCALING (For CM Use) #

    If you're using a standard 28–32mm scale (e.g. Gunpla HG kits, Battletech, etc.):

    Light mechs: 5–8 cm tall

    Medium mechs: 8–11 cm

    Heavy mechs: 11–15+ cm
    Adjust your terrain height and effects accordingly for cover and LOS purposes.

    Chapter 03

    Combat #

    🎲 COMBAT #

    Attacking #

    Choose a weapon and a valid target

    Roll a number of D6 equal to the weapon’s FP (ranged) or ME (melee)

    Compare each die to the target’s Defence (D)

    Each die equal to or higher than the target's Defence = 1 hit

    Each hit = 1 Structure damage (unless modified)

    🎯 Natural Dice Rule #

    A natural 6 always hits, even if the target's Defence is higher than the attacker's roll modifiers

    A natural 1 always misses, even if modifiers would normally make the hit succeed

    Weapon Format #

    Ranged Weapon:
    FP | Range | Keywords

    Melee Weapon:
    ME | Keywords

    Needs review · Close combat needs clarification

    The source limits which weapons may be used in melee, but does not fully define charging, engagement distance, or disengaging. No extra procedure has been added here.

    Melee Restriction:

    Ranged weapons (FP) cannot be used in melee combat.

    Only melee weapons (ME) and melee-based systems may be used in close combat.

    If a unit is engaged in melee, it may only attack with its ME stat or melee systems.

    Example:

    Rail Carbine: FP 2 | Range: 45.5 cm | Piercing 1, Accurate

    Beam Saber: ME 3 | AP 1, Cleave

    Weapon Use Restriction

    Each weapon system may only be used once per activation.

    A unit with multiple weapons may use each of them once (spending an action for each).

    If a weapon has the Rapid Fire keyword or another special rule, it will state its own exceptions.

    Chapter 04

    Actions #

    Needs review · Overwatch is not defined

    Other rules mention Overwatch, but this core document does not give its action cost, trigger, or resolution procedure.

    🧭 ACTION LIST #

    Each unit can take 2 actions per activation:

    Move – Move up to M in cm.

    Attack – Make a ranged or melee attack.

    Transform – Switch between forms (if applicable).

    Use System/Ability – Activate a system or trait.

    Braced – Gain +1 Defense until the start of the unit’s next activation. The unit may not Move during the same activation. Braced ends if the unit Moves (voluntary or forced).

    Interact – Perform an interaction with terrain or scenario objectives.

    Chapter 05

    Damage & destruction #

    💥 DAMAGE & DESTRUCTION #

    Each hit = 1 Structure lost

    At 0 Structure, the unit is destroyed

    Needs review · Destruction timing needs clarification

    The source delays removal until round end, but its wording about completing an activation is unclear. The interaction with last attacks, spawning, and revival also needs a decision.

    Delayed Destruction

    Units reduced to 0 STR remain on the battlefield until the end of the current round. They may complete their current activation if they have not already acted. This allows units to strike back or complete objectives before becoming a wreck. A destroyed unit cant contest or interact with objectives.

    🪖 COVER & MODIFIERS #

    In Cover: +1 to Defence against ranged attacks

    Chapter 06

    Morale & suppression #

    Optional module · As labeled in the source

    🚨 OPTIONAL MODULE: MORALE & SUPPRESSION #

    Trigger a Morale Test when: #

    The unit takes damage and survives

    An allied unit within 15.0 cm is destroyed

    Needs review · Morale check needs confirmation

    As written, a result below current STR causes suppression. Higher-STR units therefore fail more often. The source wording is preserved; no balance change has been made.

    Morale Test: #

    Roll 1D6
    If the result is less than current STR, the unit becomes Suppressed

    Suppressed Units: #

    Can only take 1 action on next activation

    Cannot charge

    Modifiers: #

    In cover: +1 to morale roll

    Near commander (within 15.0 cm): Reroll failed morale

    Drones/AI: Immune

    Chapter 08

    Getting started #

    🧠 DESIGN PRINCIPLES #

    Weapons define offense: FP and ME are part of the weapon, not the unit

    Alternating activation: Keep both players engaged

    Modular systems: Add morale, heat, commanders, etc. as needed

    Fast, scalable play: 2–6 units per side = 20–60 min game

    📦 GETTING STARTED #

    Choose a faction and 2–5 units

    Use terrain that offers line of sight blocking and cover

    Pick a mission or play the Quick Start scenario

    Get rolling!

    IRON STARS is designed to be fast, cinematic, and expandable. Whether you’re fielding Gunpla, Transformers, Battletech minis, or 3D prints — you’re welcome here.

    🧱 Core Concept #

    Each unit’s point cost is the sum of three elements:

    Chassis Cost – based on core stats (M, A, STR)

    Weapons Cost – based on FP/ME, range, and traits

    Traits/Systems Cost – based on passive or activated abilities

    This makes it easy to mix and match parts while keeping balance.

    🧩 4. Faction Identity & Unique Options #

    In narrative or faction-aligned games, each faction may include exclusive traits, weapons, or systems.

    Open Play Note: You may ignore faction locks in casual games if both players agree.

    📚 Keyword Glossary #

    These keywords are used in weapons, traits, and abilities throughout Iron Stars. Each one adds a special effect or modifier to an attack or system.

    ⚔️ Combat Keywords #

    ⚔️ Combat Keywords #
    KeywordEffect
    FP (Firepower)# Used only for ranged combat. Cannot be used in melee combat.
    ME (Melee)#Used only for close combat. Cannot be used for ranged attacks.
    FP and ME are never interchangeable unless a special rule explicitly allows it.
    Accurate#Add +1 to one attack die per roll (or reroll 1 failed die).
    AP X (Armor Piercing)#Reduce the target's Defence by X for this attack.
    Blast#Affects all units within 5 cm of the target point. Each is rolled against separately.
    Burn#When a weapon with Burn causes at least 1 damage, place a Burn token on the target. At the start of the target's next activation, it suffers 1 additional STR damage, then the Burn token is removed.
    Cleave#If this attack destroys a unit in melee, the attacker may immediately make one bonus melee attack against another adjacent target.
    Heavy#Cannot be used if the unit moved this turn unless the weapon is mounted (trait-specific).
    Knockback#On hit, push the target 5 cm directly away from the attacker.
    Overheat X#After resolving the attack, roll 1D6. On a 1, the attacker suffers X STR damage (usually 1).
    Piercing X#Each successful hit from this weapon deals +X additional Structure damage.
    Rapid Fire#May fire twice if the unit did not move this turn. The second shot must target the same unit.
    Suppressive#If this attack hits, the target suffers -1 FP until the end of its next activation.
    Indirect Fire (Missiles)#May target enemies without Line of Sight.

    Cannot target enemies engaged in melee.

    Apply -1 to hit when firing without LOS.
    Cover bonuses still apply to the target (e.g., +1 Defence vs ranged).

    Cost: Missile weapons with Indirect Fire cost +2 pts over standard equivalents.
    Linked#Effect:
    A Linked weapon fires all of its shots at the same target.

    Multiple identical weapons may be combined into one Linked system.

    Add their Firepower (FP) together to determine the total FP of the attack.

    The attack is resolved as a single roll against the target’s Defense
    Example
    A Cannon has FP 2.

    A Quad-Linked Cannon counts as a single weapon with FP 8.

    You roll 8 dice at once, all directed at the same targe
    Linked Costing:#When building a Linked weapon system, the first 3 identical weapons cost their normal points value.
    Beginning with the 4th linked weapon, each additional weapon costs an extra amount equal to its base cost for each step beyond the third.
    Example: a 4-point Light Laser costs 4/4/4/8/12 when taken as a 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th linked copy.
    Anti-Air (AA)#Weapons with Anti-Air gain +1 to hit Flyers.

    If a Flyer is targeted by an Anti-Air weapon, it does not benefit from Cover (but may still use Defense as normal).

    Anti-Air has no effect against non-Flyer units.
    Ammunition X#This weapon may be used X times per game.
    Each time you resolve an attack with this weapon, reduce X by 1.
    At 0, the weapon cannot be used for the rest of the game.

    Flyer#Units with the Flyer keyword are specialized aerial units that fight from above the battlefield. Their mobility is unmatched, but they are harder to keep under cover.
    🔧 Effect:
    A Flyer must move at least half of its Move value during its activation.

    If a Flyer does not move its full Move value, any attacks targeting it receive +1 to hit.

    A Flyer may draw Line of Sight (LoS) from any point above its base up to a maximum height of 61 cm.

    Weapons fired from that vantage point must still have range measured from that point to the target in order to be used.
    Flyers cannot claim or contest objectives.
    📌 Notes for Clarity
    Flyers can see over most terrain and units, but range is calculated from their “altitude vantage point.”

    They are still affected by Cover if terrain is tall enough to obscure the LoS point chosen.

    Flyers cannot stay stationary — they must always be “in motion.”

    Traits like Jetpack and Hover don’t stack with Flyer — they’re separate mobility systems.
    Tracked#This unit may not climb elevation.
    Difficult Ground movement penalties are doubled.
    This unit has the Stable Platform keyword.
    Stable Platform#This unit ignores the Heavy weapon restriction.
    It may fire Heavy weapons even if it moved this activation.
    Uniqe#A unit may include only 1 Unique Trait/System

    Secure the Relay #

    🎮 QUICK START SCENARIO — “SECURE THE RELAY” #

    Players: 2

    Map Size: 61.0 cm x 61.0 cm

    Each Player Uses: 2 Units

    Objective: Control the center objective (Relay Beacon) at the end of Round 3

    Relay Beacon (Central Objective)

    Use a round marker with a 3 cm radius (6 cm across)
    A unit controls the beacon if it is within 3 cm of the center and no enemies are also within that range
    Optional: Place a mech part, relay tower, or glowing token for visual flair.

    Scoring:

    2 VP – Control the Relay at end of Round 3

    1 VP – Destroy 1 enemy unit

    +1 Bonus VP – Destroy both enemy units

    Tie-breaker: Player controlling the Relay wins
