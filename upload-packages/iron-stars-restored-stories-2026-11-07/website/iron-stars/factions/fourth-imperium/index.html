Background

Background

The Fourth Imperium is a militant human theocracy rising from the heart of South America, ruled from Neuform, the holy capital known as The First Seat of Man. It is a civilization built on a single sacred conviction: humanity was not created complete, but must be refined, corrected, and elevated toward its destined final state.

Where other powers fight for profit, survival, or sovereignty, the Fourth Imperium wages war as a sacred obligation. Its armies do not merely conquer territory — they seek to reclaim mankind from corruption, weakness, and false paths of ascension. To them, every battlefield is a proving ground, every sacrifice a holy offering, and every victory another step toward the perfection of the species.

At the head of the Imperium stands The Ascendant, the supreme temporal and spiritual ruler judged closest to the ideal of the First Form.

Ideology

Beliefs & society

Monolithic Theocracy: Society is governed through the Doctrine of the First Form, a monotheistic state faith centered on humanity’s sacred destiny.

Perfection Through Correction: Weakness is not merely tolerated or condemned — it is something to be disciplined, purified, or expended in service to the whole.

The Individual Beneath the Species: Citizens are taught that a single life has value only insofar as it serves mankind’s collective ascent.

Measured Worth: Purity, discipline, bodily excellence, and loyalty determine status. Rule belongs not to the popular, but to the worthy.

Doctrine

Military doctrine

Standardized Holy War: The Imperium fields disciplined mass-production war machines built to carry doctrine into battle through force of arms.

Territorial Crusade: Its main offensives are directed northward against the UNMC, whose corporate rule is denounced as decadent corruption.

Close Assault & Sacrifice: While many Imperial formations advance in ordered ranks, some branches embrace martyrdom, shock assault, and close-quarters annihilation.

Measured Command: Even in war, the Imperium seeks order — its officers, judges, and synods direct battle as an extension of sacred law.

Within the faction

Subfactions

🧭 Subfactions – The Four Imperial Arms

The Northern Crusades

The largest and most politically favored branch of the Fourth Imperium.

These forces lead the great reconquest campaigns into Ecuador, Colombia, and Venezuela, pushing against UNMC strongholds and corporate enclaves. They are disciplined conquerors, built for territorial war, objective seizure, and the relentless forward grind of holy expansion.

Trait: Righteous Advance – When attacking an enemy unit that is contesting or controlling an objective, reroll 1 failed hit.

The Host of Completion

The most feared of the Imperial arms.

The Host of Completion believes true sanctity is found in nearness to death and the willing offering of the self in battle. Its assault mechs are built for brutal charges, close combat, and decisive acts of sacrificial violence. To them, distance preserves the self — and completion demands contact.

Trait: Completion Through Contact – +1 ME when charging.

The Eastern Vigil

Wardens of the oceanic frontier.

Stationed along the Imperium’s eastern approaches, the Eastern Vigil watches the sea lanes toward Africa and the unknown horrors beyond. Their forces are dour, conservative, and accustomed to hunting threats too large, too strange, or too unnatural to fit neatly inside Imperial doctrine.

Trait: Watchfire Discipline – If this unit did not move this activation, reroll 1 failed ranged hit. If the target has STR 5+ or Fly, that attack also gains AP +1.

The Synod of the First Measure

The doctrinal heart of the empire.

Drawn from Neuform and the institutions closest to the Ascendant, the Synod interprets purity law, judges worthiness, and enforces the Imperium’s sacred standard. Its mech formations are highly controlled, disciplined, and often reserved for command roles, exemplars, and the correction of heresy within the ranks.

Trait: Measured Authority – Once per round, a friendly Fourth Imperium unit within 10 cm of one or more Synod units may reroll a failed morale, suppression, or command roll.

🤖 Unit Example: Imperial Legionnaire Frame

The standard line mech of the Fourth Imperium.

A mass-produced humanoid combat frame used across the empire’s crusading armies, the Legionnaire is built around reliability, intimidation, and doctrinal uniformity. Its silhouette is stern and unmistakably martial, with a rifle for disciplined line warfare and a melee sidearm for close engagement when the advance becomes a charge.

Depending on the branch that fields it, the Legionnaire may appear as a straightforward line unit, a frontier warden, or the base pattern for more fanatical assault variants.

Typical armament:

Standard battle rifle

Combat blade or shock mace

Optional shield, breaching weapon, or doctrinal support system

Battlefield role: Core line trooper, objective pusher, doctrinal backbone
