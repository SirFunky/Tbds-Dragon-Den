Background

Background

The Iron Concord is a sprawling, militarized feudal empire forged from the ashes of old Europe and Eurasia. Ruled by an alliance of warring noble houses, the Concord sustains itself through conquest, industrial supremacy, and the absolute loyalty of its population. Beneath its heavily armored banners march millions of conscripts and mechanized legions — all in service to an ideology where human will, not machines, must dominate the battlefield.

Their war doctrine is brutal, efficient, and unrelenting. Innovation is tolerated only when it reinforces hierarchy. Artificial intelligence is outlawed, despised, and exterminated wherever found.

Ideology

Beliefs & society

Neo-Feudalism: Ruled by a High Council of noble houses — power comes from blood and steel

Anti-AI Doctrine: All synthetic intelligence is seen as heretical, a betrayal of humanity

Industrial Might: Cities are weapons-factories; people are fuel

Honor Through Obedience: Cowardice, hesitation, and failure are punished by fire

Doctrine

Military doctrine

Heavy mech dominance: Firepower, durability, and line-breaking capacity

Doctrine over flexibility: Units follow strict command chains

Attritional warfare: Grind the enemy down, hold ground at all costs

Linewalker Legions: Iconic medium/heavy mechs fielded in great numbers

On the tabletop

Strengths & weaknesses

Role
Durable frontliners, battlefield anchors
Strengths
Heavy armor, long-range firepower, morale resilience
Weaknesses
Low mobility, few tricks, anti-AI rigidity
Ideal For
Players who like slow, methodical dominance and strong faction identity

Within the faction

Subfactions

👑 Subfactions – The Great Noble Houses

House Romanow

Oldest and most powerful house, draped in ceremonial steel and tradition

Specializes in super-heavy mechs and city-breaking siege units

Trait: Iron Will – Ignore the first morale/command effect each game

House Draughan

Internal enforcers, fire-wielding fanatics and battlefield purgers

Flame-based area control and morale suppression

Trait: Scorch Doctrine – Flame weapons deal extra damage to units in cover

House Vexillis

Cunning manipulators and back-channel tacticians

Experts in sabotage, ECM warfare, and command disruption

Trait: Disruptive Presence – Enemies within 15.0 cm suffer penalties to command actions

House Kaelstrom

Young and aggressive, built on conquest and raw martial strength

Shock assault cavalry and fast-hitting frontline breakers

Trait: Storm Charge – Bonus melee attacks when charging from cover or elevation
