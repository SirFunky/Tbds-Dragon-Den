Systems, traits & special rules

Systems & traits

Radiant Halo Overcharger

Radiant Halo Overcharger – Aura: Allies within 20 cm gain +2 Morale, Overwatch, and +1 Defence.
Drawback: Each time activated, roll 1D6; on 1–2, this unit suffers 1 STR (“system burnout”).

Shield-Wings Array+

Shield-Wings Array+ – +2 Defence vs ranged when activated; leap 20 cm once per turn.
Drawback: On a natural 1 during a Defence roll, wings overload → cannot leap for the rest of the battle.

Experimental Overdrive

Experimental Overdrive – May take a 3rd action; if it’s an Attack, gain +1 to hit. On 1–2, suffer 1 STR. On 6, burns out.

Vow of Radiance+

Vow of Radiance+ – At start of game: gain both +1 ME in duels AND +1 Aura range.

Inspire by Deed+

Inspire by Deed+ – When this unit kills, all allies on the battlefield gain +1 to hit until end of round.
