Background

Background

The so-called "United Nations" is a thin veil — a democratic shell-state entirely owned and operated by a cartel of hyper-capitalist megacorporations. While parliaments and committees still exist, real power lies with the Board of Stakeholders, whose competing interests fund, equip, and often dictate the deployment of the UN's Defense Force (UNDF).

This faction combines aesthetic PR warfare, experimental weapons, and mercenary doctrine, operating with incredible tech sophistication — and incredible internal dysfunction.

Ideology

Beliefs & society

Corporatocracy: Democracy is a product sold to citizens

Privatized Warfare: Every battlefield is a boardroom play

Decentralized Command: UNDF units answer to different corporate sponsors

Results-Driven: Performance reports matter more than casualties

Doctrine

Military doctrine

Transformable Frames & Aerospace Power: Inspired by Macross-style mobility

Experimental Loadouts: Cutting-edge tech with high volatility

Contract-Driven Warfare: Units perform better near objectives or with corporate backing

Media Manipulation: Some units literally fight better when "on camera"

Within the faction

Subfactions

🏙️ Subfactions – The Big Four Megacorps

Atlas Dynamics

Private Military Industrial Titan

Specializes in armored mechs, suppressive fire, and battlefield sustainability

Trait: Hard Contracts – +1 to hit when holding or contesting an objective

Stratos Inc.

Orbital infrastructure & drone strike logistics

Dominates the air and space layer with flyers and carrier mechs

Trait: Strike from Above – Once per game, deep-strike a unit anywhere on the map

PanOptik Systems

Media, propaganda, and electronic warfare

Focus on morale control, holograms, and psychological impact

Trait: Media Spin – When this unit destroys an enemy, nearby allies gain +1 to hit next turn

SynCo Holdings

Finance and innovation conglomerate

Has stakes in all others and access to prototype tech

Trait: Black Budget – Gain 1 resource reroll token per round while holding at least 1 objective
