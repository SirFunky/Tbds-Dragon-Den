Ghost Talon and T-601’s Necrons face one another again, this time across a battlefield built around a single raised communications platform.

For the campaign, the fight returns to Kappa-9: the listening post whose records drew both forces into the first encounter. Ghost Talon still needs answers. The Necrons still stand between the T’au and the installation.

The tabletop has changed. The two bunkers are gone. A long defensive wall runs diagonally through the board, meeting the central platform. Rocky positions at opposite corners offer further elevation, while the Vox tower stands beside the main structure.

The recovered account identifies this as the player’s first Matched Play game: Awaken the Data Spirits, ending 18–8 to the Necrons. Its photo groups are labelled at the end of rounds one through four. The narrative follows those groups; individual attacks, initiative rolls and round-by-round scores were not recorded.

02 / THE ENGAGEMENT

Four rounds at the listening post.

A stronger opening for Ghost Talon gives way to an aggressive push after round two. The recovered photo sequence shows the fight tightening around the platform before the Necrons finish ahead.

SETUP / MATCHED PLAY

Awaken the Data Spirits

This is a different test from the first battle’s Loot and Salvage. The player’s recovered account calls Awaken the Data Spirits a difficult mission for stealth suits, but describes a much better start to this game.

The diagonal wall cuts through the board and meets the raised communications platform. Rocky positions anchor opposite corners, while the Vox tower offers a narrow elevated position beside the main structure.

Ghost Talon has chosen Recon and Infiltration Tac Ops. The Necron cards show a Security set. Both forces must make their objectives work around a fight that increasingly draws them onto the same roof.

ROUND 01 / END-OF-ROUND RECORD

01

The better opening

Ghost Talon makes contact with the installation. Two stealth suits occupy the near portion of the roof, while others remain on the approaches. Across the platform, Necron infantry establish their own positions.

The player remembered this opening as much better than the previous game. The end-of-round photographs show a T’au force actively contesting the structure, with suits still spread across the field rather than confined to one position.

The Necrons have also arrived in strength. The roof is already shared territory, with silver infantry at ground level beside the tower and rocky terrain.

ROUND 02 / END-OF-ROUND RECORD

02

Positions established

By the end of round two, the fight has several levels. A T’au suit occupies the Vox tower, another stands on a rocky rise, and both forces contest the raised installation.

The Necrons remain concentrated around the central array, with additional infantry covering the surrounding ground. Ghost Talon has positions from which to work, but fewer models with which to sustain the contest.

This is the point the player later identified as the hinge of the game: after round two, the T’au attack became too aggressive.

ROUND 03 / END-OF-ROUND RECORD

03

The push goes too far

Ghost Talon commits more aggressively after the second round. The account does not preserve each move or attack, but the player’s assessment is clear: the push allowed the Necrons to bring their greater number of models to bear.

The photographs at the end of round three show Necrons standing across the platform, a model fallen beside the approaches, and T’au suits operating around the outer positions.

In the Kappa-9 narrative, the advance has brought Ghost Talon close to the station’s systems. On the tabletop, the same commitment has left the cadre struggling against the Necron numbers.

ROUND 04 / END-OF-ROUND RECORD

04

The Necrons close it out

The final round leaves fallen models on the roof and beside the platform. Both sides have taken losses, but the Necrons retain a strong presence around the installation.

A stealth suit remains on the tower. Others are down near the wall and amid the central fighting. The photographs preserve the end positions, while the recovered account supplies the result: eighteen victory points for the Necrons, eight for the T’au.

The rematch breaks the first game’s deadlock. Ghost Talon began better, but the late aggression was punished, and T-601’s force takes the victory.
