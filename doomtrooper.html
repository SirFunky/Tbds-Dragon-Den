The format at a glance

What’s different?

Doomtrooper: Commander is an alternative format for the classic Doomtrooper collectible card game, introducing commanders, faction alliances, and a redesigned combat system.

The format builds upon the original game, introducing new strategic possibilities while remaining compatible with existing cards.

01

Commanders

Each player chooses a Personality card to serve as their Commander.

  • The Commander’s faction determines the deck’s primary faction.
  • Commanders begin in a dedicated Command Zone outside the deck.
  • Commanders can be played directly from the Command Zone.
  • The first deployment costs the Commander’s normal Destiny Point cost.
  • Each subsequent deployment costs an additional 2 Destiny Points for every previous deployment from the Command Zone.
  • When killed, Commanders return to the Command Zone.
1st deploymentNormal cost
2nd deploymentNormal + 2 DP
3rd deploymentNormal + 4 DP
02

Factions and deck building

The Commander’s faction determines which faction’s cards may be included in the deck.

Players may also select one subfaction from the Paradise Lost expansion.

Available subfactions

Sons of RasputinTemplarsCrescentiaLutheran Triad
  • The Dark Legion cannot include a Paradise Lost subfaction.
  • General cards remain available regardless of faction.
  • Paradise Lost warriors are treated as Doomtroopers for gameplay purposes.
03

Redesigned combat system

Combat has been redesigned to make coordinated attacks and defensive formations more effective.

Multiple blockers

  • A defending player may assign multiple warriors to block a single attacker.
  • The blocking warriors combine their combat values when defending against that attacker.

Counterattacks

  • The attacker chooses the order in which assigned blockers are attacked.
  • If the attacker’s combat value exceeds what is required to defeat a blocker, the remaining value can be applied to the next blocker.
  • This continues until all blockers have been resolved or the attacker has insufficient combat value remaining.
Assign blockers→Choose attack order→Resolve in sequence
The system rewards coordinated defense while allowing powerful warriors to fight through multiple enemies.
04

Removal of Outposts

The separate Outpost play area from Paradise Lost has been eliminated.

  • Paradise Lost warriors deploy into the normal play area.
  • Outpost-specific movement mechanics are removed.
  • Cards referencing Outposts are adjusted to accommodate the new system.
05

Warzones

When multiple warriors participate in defending a Warzone, the defending player chooses one participating warrior to receive its bonus.

Only that warrior benefits from the Warzone during the engagement.

06

Alliance cards

Alliance cards are shuffled into the main deck and must be drawn and played normally.

They do not begin the game in play or in a separate zone.

07

Missions

When a mission requires the elimination of an enemy warrior, the mission succeeds if that enemy is killed during combat in which the mission carrier participates.

The mission carrier does not need to deliver the killing blow.

Doomtrooper: Commander is an unofficial, fan-made alternative format for the original Doomtrooper collectible card game.
