What Is Eldritch Shadows?

Eldritch Shadows is a modern dark urban fantasy setting where reality is fragile and humanity lives dangerously close to forces it does not understand. When cracks open, cults grow in the shadows, or something steps in from another dimension, it is the players’ job to stop the threat, contain the damage, and make sure the world keeps functioning as if nothing happened.

The game blends cosmic horror, modern monster hunting, secret organizations, and supernatural escalation.

GDA agents Mental pressure Dark mysteries Contemporary settings

The tone is more “hold the line and survive the night” than heroic fantasy. The characters are capable, but the world is brutal: injuries break down their core attributes, mana is both a tool and a risk, and the threats are just as eager to crush the mind as the body.

Core Pillars of the Project

Dark Modernity

Shopping malls, subways, parking garages, hospitals, and darkened houses become stages for horror breaking through everyday life.

Psychological and Physical Breakdown

Damage lives in your attributes. You do not just lose “HP” — you lose control, strength, wisdom, or the ability to keep functioning.

Multiple Ways to Face the Threat

Investigation, social manipulation, monster hunting, rituals, and raw survival combat all coexist in the same campaign.

Play As…

The ruleset supports several distinct player fantasies, from hunters and protectors to ritualists and nanomancers. That lets the group feel like a real response team rather than four versions of the same hero.

How the Game Feels

Every conflict in Eldritch Shadows leaves a mark. Winning matters, but so does what survives the aftermath: the injury, the memory, the fear, and the damage no one else is allowed to remember.

Dice Pools with Clear Risk

You build rolls from a core attribute + a discipline. Every 6 is a success, but catastrophic failures can break both equipment and character.

Damage That Matters

Superficial damage can be recovered through rest, but critical damage can lead to permanent scars, lost function, and long-term consequences.

Mana, Pressure, and Sacrifice

Mana powers rerolls, magic, and powerful abilities. When it runs out, the character can literally begin burning their soul.

Two Paths to the Supernatural

Ritual magic and nanomancy exist side by side in the setting. That gives Eldritch Shadows a distinctive mix of occult horror, modern crisis response, and technological desperation.

Ritual Magic

Magic and Rituals

Water jets, fireballs, earth spikes, protective circles, blessed weapons, healing rituals, and utility magic make the magician both a problem-solver and a risk factor.

  • Elemental offensive and defensive magic
  • Rituals that take time but can turn entire scenes
  • Magic that feels costly, dangerous, and often like a last resort
Techno-Mysticism

Nanomancy

Nanomancers control programs rather than spells: swarm attacks, reconnaissance, repair, signal distortion, nanofog, and defense programs that change how the group operates.

  • CPU load creates tactical choices
  • Can repair, sabotage, and manipulate technology and the environment
  • Gives the setting a distinct modern and original profile

Start Playing

We recommend 4 players, but 1–6 players works well. You will also need one Game Master (GM). Read the core rules and your own role before your first session; use the rest of the book as a reference. See three examples of play.

What to Read First

  1. Rule 0 and Content Warning. Start with the shared goal of the game, then discuss the tone of the horror and the group's boundaries together.
  2. Conflict and Skill Checks, Attributes, and Disciplines. Learn how to build a dice pool, count successes, and use disciplines and specializations.
  3. Damage / Downed and Mana. Understand how injuries affect your attributes, what happens when an attribute reaches zero, and how to spend and recover Mana.
  4. Conflicts and Time. Read how opposed rolls, aggressive and defensive actions, and turns work.
  5. Quickstart Templates or Prebuilt Classes. Pick a template or one of the five ready-to-play characters for your first game, then read your chosen abilities and equipment. If you prefer to build your own character, follow Create Your Character.
  6. Your role's special rules. Magic users should read their relevant entries in Magic & Rituals. Nanomancers should read the Nanomancy rules and their chosen Nanomancy Programs. You can look up other powers when needed.

Keep These Nearby

Use Conflict – Quick Reference during play and Glossary to look up unfamiliar terms and conditions. Read the full conflict rules first, and return to them when you need more detail. Experience can wait until the end of your first session.

Into the Fog

Three Scenes at the Table

Follow Emanuél, Hannes, Tizian, Emilia, and Maria on a standalone mission beneath a deserted station. The player characters use their actual ready-to-play character sheets. The situation, NPCs, and dice results are invented for these examples; they reveal nothing about the Into the Fog campaign.

1. Convince a Witness

The scene. A station guard has locked the gate to a closed platform. A passenger disappeared there, but he refuses to let anyone through. Emanuél Markusson recognises the fear behind his refusal. “Someone you know is down there, isn't she? Let us look. You don't have to go back yourself.” Maria keeps the others a few steps back, giving him room to talk.

The check. Emanuél uses ordinary persuasion. The GM calls for Empathy + Social Interaction, opposed by the guard using the same combination. Emanuél's sheet gives him Empathy 4 and Social Interaction 1: 5d6, doubled by his Persuasion specialization to 10d6. The example guard has Empathy 2 and Social Interaction 1, with no matching specialization: 3d6.

The roll. Emanuél rolls 6, 6, 5, 5, 4, 3, 3, 2, 2, 1: two successes. The guard rolls 6, 4, 2: one success. His success cancels one of Emanuél's, leaving one success.

What happens. The guard unlocks the gate. “Five minutes. If you hear the announcement, don't answer it.” Emanuél has persuaded him to give the group access using his social skills. He thanks the guard and asks the others to follow.

2. Search for Clues

The scene. Beyond the gate, the platform lights flicker. Hannes Månsdottir crouches beside a discarded passenger bag, examining the dust and the marks around it. The familiar rhythm of an investigation takes over, and he stops watching the tunnel. Maria notices and covers that direction while Emilia waits to examine anything supernatural they uncover.

The check. The GM chooses Intelligence + Observe to search the area. Hannes has Intelligence 1 and Observe 1: 2d6. His Investigate specialization doubles that to 4d6. This is a normal skill check, with no opposing roll or difficulty modifier.

The roll. Hannes rolls 5, 4, 2, 1: no successes. He spends 1 Mana to reroll two dice. They come up 6, 3, leaving him with one success. His current Mana drops from 6 to 5.

What happens. Under the bag, Hannes finds a maintenance key streaked with fresh black residue. The same residue marks the handle of a service door. He photographs both before pointing out the connection. The group now has a lead to follow; understanding the residue's nature will require further investigation.

3. Fight in the Dark

The scene. The service door opens. An armored human cultist emerges with a knife. Maria Gonzales moves to shield the others while Tizian Hökstorm prepares his nanocore. The GM starts a conflict, and everyone rolls a d6 for turn order. Tizian acts before the cultist, who is within 30 meters.

The first action. Tizian activates Swarm Attack. Nanomancy uses Intelligence + Technology: his Intelligence 3 and Technology 1 give 4d6. His Programming specialization applies to running the program, doubling the pool to 8d6. The program uses 10% CPU and no Mana.

The roll. Tizian rolls 6, 5, 5, 4, 3, 2, 2, 1: one success. The program activates. Its listed effect deals 2 damage, ignoring armor. For this example, the cultist has Strength 4 and armor value 3; the armor does not reduce Swarm Attack's damage. The GM applies the damage to Strength, reducing it from 4 to 2.

The second action. Tizian moves behind Maria. His CPU in use has risen from 0% to 10%, within his 30% capacity, and his Mana remains 6. Ending the program frees its CPU load.

The next choice. The wounded cultist turns toward Emanuél. Maria is within 2 meters of her teammate and uses Bodyguard when the attack comes, swapping places with him and becoming the target. The attack must still be resolved. Emilia's turn is still to come, and the conflict continues.

Safety and License

⚠ Content Warning

Eldritch Shadows includes psychological trauma, horror, violence, corruption, and disturbing supernatural themes. It is built for groups who want tension, dread, and meaningful consequences.

  • Discuss boundaries before play
  • Use safety tools that fit your table
  • Adjust tone and intensity to suit your group

Open Rules, Protected Setting

The rules are shared under ESOL, while the setting, names, world-specific lore, and visual identity remain protected. That keeps the system open for use while preserving what makes Eldritch Shadows its own world.

  • Rules and systems can be reused under the license
  • Attribution matters
