Beta notes for the GM

The printed class presets are preserved. These notes identify places where the beta needs a ruling; they do not add new abilities or campaign equipment.

Hannes Månsdottir - Hunter

Use the class loadout rather than a new 60-point shopping budget. The class provides no special ammunition; use normal rounds unless the GM awards or approves other ammunition. Follow the weapon rules for dual-wielding modifiers.

Emanuél Markusson - Inquisitor

The Upper Arm Blade has 2 rune slots. Its Sharp Rune uses both slots and adds +1 damage because the weapon already deals Slashing damage: 3 + 1 = 4. This equipment follows the listed rune rules.

Tizian Hökstorm - Nanomancer

Nanomancy uses Intelligence + Technology and has no Mana cost. CPU capacity is Intelligence x 10% = 30%. The overload limit is 100% of that personal capacity: three programs at 10% fill Tizian's CPU. At full capacity, attempting another program requires a Nanomancy roll; failure causes 1 Wisdom damage and Paralyzed for 1 round. The author has corrected the class weapon to Short Sword + Sharp Rune: its 2 rune slots hold the rune, and damage is 3 + 1 = 4 Slashing.

Emilia Olsson - Mage

The author has corrected the Mage to 3 discipline points. Emilia has Occult 2 (Magic) and Academia 1 (Chemistry); the extra Medicine 1 (Triage) entry has been removed. The laptop's three rituals are item access, not extra purchased abilities.

Maria Gonzales - Protector

Ki requires a discipline tied to the chosen element. Maria's printed class lists Occult (Spirits); the GM should confirm which elements it supports. The class lists several armor-granting items without a clear stacking rule. Their individual values are recorded; no combined armor total is assumed.

All five characters begin with 12 attribute points, 6 Mana, no injuries and zero earned XP. All five characters have 3 discipline points, including the author-corrected Mage. Equipment is provided by the class, rather than bought with an additional 60-point allowance.
