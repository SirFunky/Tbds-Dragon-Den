About TBD's Dragon Den

A little about me, my creative work, and the worlds taking shape in the Den.

Who is behind TBD’s Dragon Den?

I’m Niklas Karlsson, a software developer by education and a creator in my spare time. My projects range from games like Eldritch Shadows to song lyrics—whatever gives me an idea worth exploring.

What is TBD’s Dragon Den?

TBD’s Dragon Den is a home for my ongoing projects and creative experiments—a place to share what I’m working on and the things I enjoy making.

How did you get into creating worlds, stories, and games?

I’ve had a vivid imagination for as long as I can remember. I started writing stories at a young age, but building the worlds around them was always what fascinated me most.

What inspires you?

Inspiration comes from all around me, especially the games I play and the books I read. I’ve always felt that I don’t live just one life—I live thousands. Every world I explore gives me something new to think about and brings fresh ideas into my own work.

What do you hope people will experience through your projects?

I want to give people the chance to explore different worlds and situations through perspectives they might not have considered before—something that sparks their curiosity and makes the experience memorable.

What’s your vision for Dragon Den?

I want Dragon Den to grow into a living hub of creative projects—a central meeting point for dozens of different universes, with new worlds and stories to discover as the site evolves.
