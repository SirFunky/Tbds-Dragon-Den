Guilds And Dragons · Book One

A Dragon Among Mortals

When an armored stranger falls from the sky into the world of Aleris, a band of adventurers discovers that he is far more than he appears. Shade is a dragon in human form, sent to understand mortal lives. Joining the adventurers’ guild brings dangerous jobs, unexpected friendships, and the challenge of finding a place among people who do not know his true nature.
