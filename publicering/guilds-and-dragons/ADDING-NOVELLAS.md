# Guilds And Dragons

The collection page guilds-and-dragons.html links to a dedicated page for each novella:

- guilds-and-dragons/book-one.html: A Dragon Among Mortals, with a short summary and 46 chapter links.
- guilds-and-dragons/book-two.html: The Dragon’s Hearth, with a short summary and 43 chapter links.

Each chapter link opens the corresponding PDF at its starting page. PDF page numbers include front matter and may differ from printed page numbers. Each book page also has full-PDF and download links. Some PDF viewers may not support page fragments.

PDFs are unchanged copies of the supplied manuscripts. If replacing a PDF, check every chapter link because pagination may change. Shared page styles are in series.css and ook.css.

The purple, crimson, and gold theme uses the supplied Book One artwork. Book Two uses its original cover, extracted from the supplied PDF as the-dragons-hearth-cover.png, on the collection and book pages.
