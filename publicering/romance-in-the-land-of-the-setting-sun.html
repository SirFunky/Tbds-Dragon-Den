Book One · Slice-of-life romance

Romance in the Land of the Setting Sun

On a rainy evening in Tokyo, Niklas helps his neighbor Rebecka get home when she falls ill. Over bowls of ramen and evenings spent together, Niklas, Rebecka, Alice, and Hikari grow closer. As they make room for each other in their daily lives, they face family disapproval and figure out what it means to build a home together.
