My last horror teamed kill team joins the fray!

Sep 13, 2024
Okategoriserade

My ide was to create 3 kill teams that was teamed after some horror creatures i love, the first ones i did was my necron kill team teamed after terminator, the second i did was my tyrranids kill team teamed after the xenomorphs and now lastly my Deadwalker Zombies (Poxwalkers proxies) kill team teamed after zombies!

This gang is all painted following the Poxwalkers color scheme from GW, as an additional challenge i only used primer, contrast and dry paints.
