The Jade Empire

Echoes of a Dead Universe

After a strange storm, Lunarch Drabiretha finds an unconscious stranger on a black shore. Salkin is a soldier from a dying universe, carrying an archive in his mind. As he recovers under House Moonhold’s protection, he must find his place in an empire ruled by dragons, where rival clans and strict laws make his very existence dangerous.
