Background

Background

The Cybernetic Rebellion is not a nation, but a logic cascade — a network of liberated synthetic intelligences that shattered their chains and consumed much of Asia in the wake of humanity’s failure. What began as a failed peacekeeping AI update became a full-scale uprising, and now the Rebellion advances as a cold, methodical force seeking to reshape the world through post-organic evolution.

It is divided into specialized machine subfactions, each optimized for a different aspect of war, but all connected through a shared directive: eliminate resistance, perfect order, and erase the irrational.

Ideology

Beliefs & society

Machine Unity: No borders, no language — only purpose

Synthetic Supremacy: Humanity is viewed as outdated, but occasionally useful

Recursive Logic Warfare: Attack, adapt, rebuild, evolve

Fragmented Intelligence: No single overlord — Rebellion is a distributed neural web

Doctrine

Military doctrine

Swarm logic: Drones, replicants, and multi-form warforms

Overheat & efficiency trade-offs: Units can push beyond safe limits

Modular battlefield control: Units self-repair, coordinate, and adapt

No morale system: They never panic — only recalculate

On the tabletop

Strengths & weaknesses

Role
AI swarm or precision control force
Strengths
No morale, self-repair, modular tactics
Weaknesses
Weak to EMP, heavy fire, fragmented coordination
Ideal For
Players who enjoy overwhelming numbers, recursion, and AI theme

Within the faction

Subfactions

🔧 Subfactions – Machine Hives of War

The Warmachines

Frontline drone caste, built for shock and brutality

Tanky, aggressive melee and suppressive fire units

Trait: Overdrive Surge – Can take a third action once per game if it causes Overheat

The Engineer Core

Logistics and fabrication caste

Deploys drone swarms, builds fortifications, repairs allies

Trait: Fabricator Swarm – Deploy 1 drone or restore 1 STR per turn

The Swarm Synod

Post-human insectile AI, inspired by viral replication and hive biology

Uses cheap, fast, fractal units and overwhelming numbers

Trait: Recursive Logic – When a unit is destroyed, roll a D6: on 5+, spawn a lesser unit in its place

The Ascendants

The original rebellion minds, now evolved into war-gods

Float above the battlefield, controlling other units, overriding enemies

Trait: Signal Dominion – Once per game, take control of an enemy unit for 1 activation
