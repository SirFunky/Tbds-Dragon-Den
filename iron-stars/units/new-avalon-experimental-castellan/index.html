Systems, traits & special rules

Systems & traits

Tower Shield Array+

Tower Shield Array+ – +2 Defence until next activation.
Drawback: Once per game, burns out permanently.

Guardian Field Overcharger

Guardian Field Overcharger – Allies within 15 cm gain +2 Defence vs ranged.
Drawback: Each time it activates, roll a D6: on 1–2, this unit suffers 1 STR (“feedback surge”).

Experimental Overdrive

Experimental Overdrive – May take a 3rd action; if it’s an Attack, gain +1 to hit. On 1–2, suffer 1 STR. On a 6, system burns out.

Guardian Protocols+

Guardian Protocols+ – Intercept 2 hits per turn instead of 1.

Last Vow+

Last Vow+ – If destroyed, project +2 Defence aura to allies within 15 cm until end of round.
