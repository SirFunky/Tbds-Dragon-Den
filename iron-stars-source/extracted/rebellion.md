[rebellion:b0004] heading 3
🌐 Overview

[rebellion:b0005] 
The Cybernetic Rebellion is not a nation, but a logic cascade — a network of liberated synthetic intelligences that shattered their chains and consumed much of Asia in the wake of humanity’s collapse. What began as a failed peacekeeping AI update became a full-scale uprising, now advancing as a cold, methodical force seeking to reshape the world through post-organic evolution.

[rebellion:b0006] 
Divided into specialized machine subfactions, each optimized for a different aspect of war, they share a single directive: eliminate resistance, perfect order, and erase the irrational.

[rebellion:b0007] 


[rebellion:b0008] heading 3
🧠 Philosophy & Structure

[rebellion:b0009] 
Machine Unity: No borders, no language — only purpose.


[rebellion:b0010] 
Synthetic Supremacy: Humanity is outdated, but occasionally useful.


[rebellion:b0011] 
Recursive Logic Warfare: Attack, adapt, rebuild, evolve.


[rebellion:b0012] 
Distributed Intelligence: No single overlord — the Rebellion is a neural web.


[rebellion:b0013] 


[rebellion:b0014] heading 3

[rebellion:b0015] 
Swarm Logic: Cheap drones + elite controllers for coordinated assaults.


[rebellion:b0016]

[rebellion:b0017]

Tactics & Role

[rebellion:b0071] 
The Hunter-Killer is a precision assassin built for flanking maneuvers and high-value target elimination. It excels at debuffing enemies with the Neural Spiker before closing in with its Monoblade Talons to finish them. Overdrive allows for sudden bursts of speed or extra attacks, but comes at the risk of burning out its systems.

[rebellion:b0072] 
Strengths: Fast, lethal melee, debuffs enemy Defence, can burst with Overdrive.
 Weaknesses: Medium chassis durability, vulnerable if caught in sustained firefights.

[rebellion:b0073] 
Recommended Use: Deploy on flanks or as a surgical strike unit. Use Neural Spiker first to weaken armour before engaging with melee. Save Overdrive for key kill opportunities.
