[avalon:b0004] heading 2
🛰️ FACTION IDENTITY

[avalon:b0005] 
Theme: Ultra-elite techno-knights, wielding perfected prototypes and unstable experimental systems.


[avalon:b0006] 
Playstyle: Tiny armies (1–3 units), but each frame is devastatingly strong.


[avalon:b0007] 
Strengths: Best Defence, unmatched accuracy, lethal in duels.


[avalon:b0008] 
Weaknesses: Outnumbered, no expendables, punished by swarms.

[avalon:b0009] 


[avalon:b0010] heading 2
🧬 CORE FACTION RULES

[avalon:b0011] 
Knightly Perfection (Universal)

[avalon:b0012] 
Each New Avalon unit may reroll 1 attack die and 1 defence die per activation.

[avalon:b0013] 


[avalon:b0014] heading 2
🛡️ Knightly Orders (Optional Subfactions)

[avalon:b0015] 
When building your army, you may choose to assign no Order, or pick one Order for your entire force.

[avalon:b0016] 
If you choose an Order:

[avalon:b0017] 
All Order-specific units in your list must belong to that chosen Order.


[avalon:b0018] 
Every unit with that Order keyword gains the Order’s trait.


[avalon:b0019] 
You may include general New Avalon units as normal, but these units do not benefit from the Order trait.


[avalon:b0020] 
You cannot mix multiple Orders in the same list.


[avalon:b0021] 
If you do not choose an Order:

[avalon:b0022] 
Your force is treated as a general New Avalon detachment.


[avalon:b0023] 
You may take general units as normal, but you do not gain any Order trait.

[avalon:b0024] 


[avalon:b0025] 
⚔️ Black Knights – The Iron Blade
 Relic Might++: Stationary = +1 ME & AP +1. In melee duels, reroll all failed hits.


[avalon:b0026] 
🛡️ Argent Aegis – The Shield Eternal
 Guardian Protocols++: Intercept unlimited hits within 10 cm. Once/game, sacrifice 2 STR to keep an ally alive at 1 STR.


[avalon:b0027] 
🎯 Luminous Lance – The Spear of Heaven
 Piercing Judgment++: Ranged ignores cover & halves Defence from systems. Once/game, one “Judgment Shot” = FP +1, AP +1, reroll all misses.


[avalon:b0028] 
🕵️ Silver Veil – The Phantom’s Oath
 Veil Phase++: Cancel first two hits on each unit. Once/game, a Veilblade may become untargetable until next activation.

[avalon:b0030] 
⚔️ RECOMMENDED UNIT ROLES

[avalon:b0031] 
Knight-Errant – Agile melee skirmisher (duel bonuses, some ranged capability)


[avalon:b0032] 
Black Knight – Heavy frontline duelist (Cleave, tanky, brutal when stationary)


[avalon:b0033] 
Veilblade Operative – Stealth assassin (phase evasion, flank killer)


[avalon:b0034] 
Lancer-Archon – Precision sniper (beam lance, duel target hunting)


[avalon:b0035] 
Aegis Castellan – Defensive tank (intercepts damage, aura defense)


[avalon:b0036] 
Seraphim Exemplar – Commander duelist (buff auras, blade mastery)


[avalon:b0037] 


[avalon:b0038] 
🎯 TACTICAL GUIDANCE

[avalon:b0039] 
Focus on synergy: Keep command units near duelists to maximize buffs.


[avalon:b0040] 
Avoid being swarmed: Most units shine in 1v1 engagements.


[avalon:b0041] 
Use orders intentionally: Pick the right Order trait for each unit’s role.


[avalon:b0042] 
Don’t overbuild ranged: New Avalon’s strength is in the blade.


[avalon:b0043] 


[avalon:b0044] 
🗡️ SAMPLE STRATEGY

[avalon:b0045] 
Pair a Knight-Errant with an Aegis Castellan — the Castellan protects with Guardian Protocols while the Errant flanks. Position a Lancer-Archon to duel the enemy’s ace from range while a Seraphim Exemplar radiates buffs to all frontline knights. Use Veilblades to strike at key targets or grab objectives with stealth and speed.
