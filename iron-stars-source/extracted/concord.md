⚙️ IRON CONCORD – FACTION RULES
 “Strength is duty. Order is war. Obedience is everything.”

[concord:b0002] heading 2
🛰️ FACTION IDENTITY

[concord:b0003] 
Faction Type: Horde-style attrition force with noble elite leaders
 Theme: Massed conscript mechs, brutal firepower, feudal discipline
 Playstyle: High model count, layered durability, morale control
 Strengths: Swarm units, suppression, heavy armor, morale buffs
 Weaknesses: Low mobility, vulnerable to elite duelists, rigid tactics

[concord:b0004] heading 3
🧬 CORE FACTION RULE: MARCH OF OBEDIENCE
 Once per game, during any turn, you may activate two Ironsworn or Auxilia units back-to-back instead of alternating.

[concord:b0005] heading 3
🛡️ NOBLE HOUSES (OPTIONAL SUBFACTION RULE)

[concord:b0006] 
The Iron Concord is ruled by feuding noble houses, each with its own battlefield legacy.

[concord:b0007] 
If you choose a House:

[concord:b0008] 
All units must belong to that House.


[concord:b0009] 
Each unit gains that House’s trait.


[concord:b0010] 
You may not mix Houses in the same force.


[concord:b0011] 
If no House is chosen:

[concord:b0012] 
Your detachment uses the generic Iron Concord rules with no House trait.

[concord:b0020] heading 3
🎯 TACTICAL GUIDANCE

[concord:b0021] 
Use Ironsworn in squads for objective control and meat-grinder zones.


[concord:b0022]

[concord:b0023]

[concord:b0024]
