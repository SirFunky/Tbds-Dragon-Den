[unmc:b0004] heading 3
🏢 Overview

[unmc:b0005] 
The so-called "United Nations" is now a sleek, corporate puppet—a patchwork of branding, politics, and profit margins. Behind the scenes, a coalition of megacorps pulls the strings through the Board of Stakeholders. Warfare is not a tragedy—it’s a performance. UNDF units are sleek, transformable, and saturated with high-tech tools designed to maximize “operational synergy.”

[unmc:b0006] 


[unmc:b0007] 
💼 Philosophy & Structure

[unmc:b0008] 
Corporatocracy: Public institutions are marketing fronts.


[unmc:b0009] 
Decentralized Command: Each mech is backed by a different corporate sponsor.


[unmc:b0010] 
Performance Over Loyalty: Metrics > morality.


[unmc:b0011] 
Marketing Warfare: Mechs are as much propaganda as they are weapons.


[unmc:b0012] 


[unmc:b0013] 
⚔️ Military Doctrine

[unmc:b0014] 
Aerospace Dominance: Mechs transform into high-speed jets or strikers.


[unmc:b0015] 
Synergistic Targeting: Units gain bonuses when coordinating with others.


[unmc:b0016] 
Objective-Driven Efficiency: Bonuses while securing assets or delivering “PR wins.”


[unmc:b0017] 
Fragile but Flashy: Tech-heavy but not built for grindfests.
