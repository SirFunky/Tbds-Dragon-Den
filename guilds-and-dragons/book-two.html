Guilds And Dragons · Book Two

The Dragon’s Hearth

Shade has found a home with Arcebek, Rakihi, and Elcia, but life among mortals is growing more complicated. As Lana draws him into the work of running the guild, he must learn to lead as well as fight. Guild politics, the demands of the Fey, and his ties to the Blue Flight test the life and relationships he is building.
